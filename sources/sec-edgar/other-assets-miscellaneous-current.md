title: Other Assets, Miscellaneous, Current — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure indicates the amount of miscellaneous assets that a publicly
  traded company expects to realize or consume within one year or the longer of its
  operating cycle. It focuses specifically on current assets, distinguishing them
  from noncurrent assets that are expected to be realized over a longer timeframe.
  This measure is narrower than the broader category of miscellaneous assets, as it
  specifically addresses those that are short-term in nature. The value is reported
  in currency as an instant value.
