title: Assets, Noncurrent — SEC EDGAR
description: Assets, Noncurrent is the total carrying amount of all assets held by
  a publicly traded company that are expected to be realized in cash, sold, or consumed
  beyond one year or the normal operating cycle. This measure provides insight into
  the long-term asset base of the company, distinguishing it from current assets,
  which are expected to be realized within a shorter timeframe. The value is reported
  in currency as of the balance sheet date.
