  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure indicates the percentage increase or decrease in fund value,
  assuming reinvestment of dividends and capital gains, while excluding gains from
  investments that do not meet the fund's investment guidelines. It is relevant to
  publicly traded investment companies and provides a clearer picture of performance
  without the influence of non-compliant investments. This measure is distinct from
  other total return measures as it specifically excludes gains from guideline violations.
  The value is reported as a percentage for the fiscal year.
