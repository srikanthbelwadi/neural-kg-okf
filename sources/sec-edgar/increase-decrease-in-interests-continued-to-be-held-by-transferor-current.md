description: This measure captures the increase or decrease in the value of assets
  created and retained during the securitization of trade receivables for a publicly
  traded company. It reflects changes in the value of interests that the company continues
  to hold after securitization, providing insight into the impact of securitization
  on asset management. This measure is specific to retained interests and differs
  from overall asset measures that do not focus on securitized assets. The amount
  is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
