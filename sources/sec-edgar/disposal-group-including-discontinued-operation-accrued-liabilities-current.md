  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reports the amount classified as accrued liabilities that
  are associated with a disposal group, which is a collection of assets and liabilities
  held for sale or already disposed of by a publicly traded company. It specifically
  focuses on liabilities expected to be settled within one year or the normal operating
  cycle, if longer. This measure is distinct from other liabilities as it pertains
  only to those related to disposal groups, providing a snapshot of short-term obligations
  in this context. The value is reported in currency as an instant value.
