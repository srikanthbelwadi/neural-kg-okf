title: Interest Expense, Debt, Excluding Amortization — SEC EDGAR
description: Interest Expense, Debt, Excluding Amortization represents the portion
  of interest incurred on debt arrangements that is charged against earnings, excluding
  any amortization of debt discounts or premiums. This measure is relevant to publicly
  traded companies and their interest obligations. It is distinguished from other
  interest expense measures by specifically excluding amortization effects, focusing
  solely on the interest charged. The reported value is in currency for the fiscal
  year.
