description: Public Utilities Interim Rate Increase (Decrease) Amount reflects the
  total dollar amount of an interim rate increase or decrease that a public utility
  is allowed while awaiting a final decision from a regulatory agency. This measure
  is important for understanding temporary financial adjustments, distinguishing it
  from final approved amounts which are subject to regulatory review. The value is
  reported in currency and reflects the duration of the interim request.
