description: Amount of real estate which is available for sale in the form of shared
  property ownership in which rights vest in several owners (buyers, timeshare holders)
  to use the property for a specified period each year (timeshare). Included in this
  item would be real estate assets in which timeshares are for sale, such as in condominiums
  at timeshare resorts. Excluded from this item would be assets, other than real estate,
  in which the Entity has sold or intends to sell timeshares, such as for cruises,
  recreational vehicles, campgrounds, aircraft (fractional ownership) and other types
  of rights.
