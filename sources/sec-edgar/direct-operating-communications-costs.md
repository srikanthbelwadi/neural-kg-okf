description: Direct Operating Communications Costs measures the communications costs
  that are directly related to goods produced and sold, as well as services rendered
  during the reporting period. This measure is relevant to publicly traded companies

  costs as it specifically focuses on those directly tied to operational activities.
  The amount is reported as a duration value in currency.
