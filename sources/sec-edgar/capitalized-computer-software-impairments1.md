description: This measure indicates the amount of impairment loss for capitalized
  costs related to software intended for sale, lease, or marketing. It describes the
  financial impact on a publicly traded company or SEC filer when the value of such

  as it specifically excludes internal-use software, focusing on marketable software.
  The amount is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
