description: Retail Related Inventory, Merchandise reports the carrying amount of
  merchandise inventory that a retailer, wholesaler, or distributor has purchased
  and is holding for future sale as of the balance sheet date. This measure specifically
  pertains to the inventory held by entities engaged in retail or wholesale activities.
  It is distinct from broader inventory measures as it focuses solely on merchandise,
  excluding raw materials or work-in-progress items. The value is reported as an instant
  value in currency.
