title: Cost Basis — Other Assets — IRS Form 990 (Nonprofit)
description: Cost or other basis of other assets sold reflects the original cost or
  basis of assets that are not classified as securities and have been sold by a nonprofit
  organization. This measure is specific to non-securities asset transactions and
  does not include costs related to securities or operational expenses. It is distinct
  from gain/loss on other assets sales, as it focuses on the cost rather than the
  financial outcome of the sale. The data is reported per organization for each fiscal
  year, in dollars.
