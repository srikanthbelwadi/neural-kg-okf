description: Land Available for Development indicates the carrying amount of land
  owned by a publicly traded company that is designated for future development projects.
  This measure specifically focuses on land that is not currently developed but is
  intended for such purposes, distinguishing it from land that is already developed
  or held for sale. The value is reported as an instant value in currency.
