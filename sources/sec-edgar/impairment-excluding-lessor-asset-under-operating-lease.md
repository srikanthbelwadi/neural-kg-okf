title: Long-Lived Asset, Held and Used, Excluding Lessor Asset under Operating Lease,
  Impairment Loss — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reflects the amount of impairment loss for long-lived assets
  held and used by a publicly traded company or SEC filer, excluding the lessor's
  underlying asset for which the right to use has been conveyed to the lessee under
  an operating lease. It specifically focuses on impairment losses related to the
  company's own long-lived assets. This measure is distinct from total impairment
  measures that may include lessor assets. The value is reported as a duration value
  in currency.
