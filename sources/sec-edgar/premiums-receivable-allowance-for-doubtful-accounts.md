description: This measure represents the estimated valuation allowance for doubtful
  accounts related to premiums receivable, as of the balance sheet date for a publicly
  traded company. It reflects the amount set aside to account for potential losses
  on premiums that may not be collectible, thus providing a more accurate picture
  of the company's financial health. This measure is distinct from gross premiums
  receivable, as it specifically addresses the estimated losses rather than the total
  receivables. The value is reported as an instant value in currency.
