title: Nonoperating Gains (Losses) — SEC EDGAR
description: Nonoperating Gains (Losses) reports the total amount of gains or losses
  that arise from activities not related to the core operations of a publicly traded
  company. This measure includes items such as interest and dividend revenue, as well
  as losses from impairments of property, plant, and equipment. It distinguishes itself
  from other financial measures by focusing specifically on nonoperating activities
  rather than operational performance. The value is reported in currency for the fiscal
  year.
