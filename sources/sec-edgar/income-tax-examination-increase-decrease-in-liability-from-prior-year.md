title: Income Tax Examination, Increase (Decrease) in Liability from Prior Year —
  SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reports the change in the liability recorded for income
  tax examinations from the previous year, indicating whether it has increased or
  decreased during the current fiscal year. It pertains to a publicly traded company
  and reflects adjustments made to tax liabilities based on examinations by tax authorities.
  This measure is distinct from other tax-related measures as it specifically focuses
  on the year-over-year change in liability rather than the total liability or other
  tax expenses. The value is reported in currency.
