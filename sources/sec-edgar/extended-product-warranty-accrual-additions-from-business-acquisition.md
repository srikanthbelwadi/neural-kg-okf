description: Extended Product Warranty Accrual, Increase from Business Combination
  measures the increase in liability for extended product warranties acquired through
  a business combination. This measure is relevant to publicly traded companies involved
  in mergers or acquisitions and reflects the financial impact of such transactions
  on warranty liabilities. It is distinct from other warranty measures as it specifically
  pertains to liabilities acquired in business combinations. The amount is reported
  in currency for the fiscal year.
