title: Gain (Loss) on Condemnation — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reports the pre-tax gain or loss resulting from the condemnation
  of property, plant, or equipment for a publicly traded company. It represents the
  difference between the net condemnation award received and the adjusted basis of
  the condemned property. This measure is distinct from other asset disposition measures
  as it specifically pertains to condemnation events rather than voluntary sales or
  disposals. The value is reported as a duration value in currency.
