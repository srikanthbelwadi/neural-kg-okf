title: Regulated Operating Revenue, Other — SEC EDGAR
description: Regulated Operating Revenue, Other reports the amount of other operating
  revenues that are recognized by a publicly traded company during a specific fiscal
  period. This measure focuses on revenues that fall under regulatory oversight but
  do not fit into more specific categories like pipelines or steam. It is distinct
  from other regulated operating revenue measures by encompassing a broader range
  of revenue sources. The value is reported in currency and reflects the duration
  of the reporting period.
