description: Payments for Proceeds from Mortgage Deposits reports the net cash inflow
  or outflow related to advance payments made to indicate the intention to complete
  a mortgage. This measure specifically pertains to publicly traded companies and
  their financial activities regarding mortgage deposits. It is distinct from other
  cash flow measures as it focuses solely on mortgage-related transactions, rather
  than general cash flows or other types of deposits. The reporting is done in currency
  for each fiscal year.
