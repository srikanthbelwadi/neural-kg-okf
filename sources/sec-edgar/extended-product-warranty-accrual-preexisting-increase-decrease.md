description: Extended Product Warranty Accrual, Increase (Decrease) for Preexisting
  Warranties measures the changes in estimates attributable to preexisting product
  warranties, indicating an increase or decrease in the accrual. This measure is relevant
  to publicly traded companies with existing warranty obligations and reflects adjustments
  based on updated estimates. It is distinct from other warranty measures as it specifically
  pertains to preexisting warranties rather than new ones. The amount is reported
  in currency for the fiscal year.
