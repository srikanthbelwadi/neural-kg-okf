title: Payments for (Proceeds from) Other Interest Earning Assets — SEC EDGAR
description: Payments for Proceeds from Other Interest Earning Assets indicates the
  net cash inflow or outflow resulting from changes in interest-earning assets that
  are not separately disclosed. This measure applies to publicly traded companies
  and encompasses a variety of financial instruments that generate interest income.
  It is distinct from other interest-related measures by including only those assets
  not specifically categorized elsewhere, providing a broader view of interest-earning
  activities. The reporting is in currency for each fiscal year.
