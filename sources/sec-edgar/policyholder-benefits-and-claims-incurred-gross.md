description: Policyholder Benefits and Claims Incurred, Direct indicates the total
  amount of expense related to the provision for policy benefits and costs incurred
  before considering the effects of policies assumed or ceded. This measure is relevant
  to publicly traded insurance companies and provides insight into their gross obligations
  to policyholders. It differs from net measures as it does not account for reinsurance
  effects. The amount is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
