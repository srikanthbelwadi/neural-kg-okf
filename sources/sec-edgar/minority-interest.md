description: Equity, Attributable to Noncontrolling Interest reflects the amount of
  equity or deficit that is attributable to noncontrolling interests in a subsidiary.
  This measure is relevant for publicly traded companies that have subsidiaries with
  minority shareholders. It is distinct from total equity measures as it specifically
  focuses on the portion of equity not owned by the parent company, excluding temporary
  equity. The value is reported as an instant value in currency.
