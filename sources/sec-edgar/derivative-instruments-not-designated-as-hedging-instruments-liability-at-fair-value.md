description: This measure indicates the fair value of derivative instruments that
  are not designated as hedging instruments and are classified as liabilities on the
  balance sheet. It is relevant to a publicly traded company or SEC filer and provides
  insight into the financial obligations associated with these derivatives at a specific
  point in time. Unlike other measures, this one specifically addresses the liability
  aspect, rather than gains, losses, or overall derivative performance. The value
  is reported in currency as an instant value.
