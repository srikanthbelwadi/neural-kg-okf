description: This measure reports the change in members' equity resulting from unit-based
  payment arrangements issued by a limited liability company (LLC). It specifically
  captures the financial impact of such arrangements on the equity of the LLC, reflecting
  how these payments affect the overall value held by members. This measure is distinct
  from other equity-related metrics as it focuses solely on unit-based compensation,
  rather than general equity changes or other forms of compensation. The value is
  reported in currency for the fiscal year.
