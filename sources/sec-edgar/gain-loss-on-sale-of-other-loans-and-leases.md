title: Gain (Loss) on Sale of Other Loans and Leases — SEC EDGAR
description: The gains (losses) included in earnings that represent the difference
  between the sale price and the carrying value of other loans and leases that were
  sold during the reporting period; excludes gains (losses) on sales of consumer and
  mortgage loans. This element refers to the gain (loss) and not to the cash proceeds
  of the sales. This element is a noncash adjustment to net income when calculating
  net cash generated by operating activities using the indirect method.
