description: Payments to Noncontrolling Interests captures the cash outflow made to
  noncontrolling interests, which are shareholders that do not have a controlling
  stake in the company. This measure is relevant to publicly traded companies and
  includes various forms of payments, such as ownership reductions, but does not include
  dividends. It is differentiated from other payment measures by its specific focus
  on noncontrolling interests rather than general shareholder payments. The amount
  is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
