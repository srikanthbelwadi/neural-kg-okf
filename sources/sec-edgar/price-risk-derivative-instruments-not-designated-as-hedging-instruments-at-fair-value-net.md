  at Fair Value, Net indicates the net fair value of all price risk derivatives that
  a publicly traded company holds but has not designated as hedging instruments. This
  measure highlights the company's exposure to price risk without the mitigating effects
  of hedging. It is reported as an instant value in currency, distinguishing it from
  other derivative measures that may not be netted.
