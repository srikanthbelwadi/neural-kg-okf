title: Other Restricted Assets — SEC EDGAR
description: Other Restricted Assets indicates the total assets that are restricted
  from use for operating purposes due to contractual or regulatory requirements extending
  beyond one year. This measure is relevant to publicly traded companies and highlights
  assets that cannot be utilized freely. It is distinguished from other asset measures
  by its specific focus on restrictions that last longer than one year. The amount
  is reported in currency as of the balance sheet date.
