title: Machinery and Equipment, Gross — SEC EDGAR
description: Machinery and Equipment, Gross reports the total value of tangible personal
  property that a publicly traded company uses to produce goods and services. This
  includes various assets such as tools, dies, molds, and office equipment. It is
  distinguished from net values by not accounting for accumulated depreciation, providing
  a snapshot of the gross investment in machinery and equipment. The measure is reported
  as an instant value in currency for the organization per fiscal year.
