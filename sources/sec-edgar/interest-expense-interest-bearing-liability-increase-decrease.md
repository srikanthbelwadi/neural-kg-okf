  liabilities for a publicly traded company or SEC filer. It reports the increase
  or decrease in costs associated with various forms of interest-bearing debt, providing
  insight into the company's borrowing costs. This measure is distinct from other
  interest expense measures as it specifically focuses on the change in interest expense,
  rather than the total amount or other aspects of interest-bearing liabilities. The
  value is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
