  Noncash is defined as transactions during a period that affect recognized assets

  "Part noncash" refers to that portion of the transaction not resulting in cash receipts
  or cash payments in the period. Nonmonetary assets and liabilities are assets and
  liabilities that will not result in cash receipts or cash payments in the future.
