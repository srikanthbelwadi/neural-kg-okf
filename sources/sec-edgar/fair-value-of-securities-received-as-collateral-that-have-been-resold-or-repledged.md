title: Fair Value of Securities Received as Collateral that Have Been Resold or Repledged
  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure captures the fair value of securities that a publicly traded
  company has received as collateral and has already been re-pledged, delivered, or
  otherwise utilized. It provides insight into the extent to which the company has
  leveraged its collateral for operational or financial purposes. This measure is
  distinct from the fair value of securities received as collateral that can be resold,
  as it specifically accounts for those that have already been used. The value is
  reported as an instant value in currency.
