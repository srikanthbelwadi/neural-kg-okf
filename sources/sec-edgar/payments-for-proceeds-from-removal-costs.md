description: Payments for Proceeds from Removal Costs reports the net cash inflow
  or outflow associated with the costs incurred for disposing of plant assets, including
  demolition, dismantling, or sale. This measure is applicable to publicly traded
  companies managing physical assets and reflects their financial obligations related
  to asset removal. It is distinct from other cost measures as it specifically addresses
  removal costs rather than general operational expenses. The reporting is in currency
  for each fiscal year.
