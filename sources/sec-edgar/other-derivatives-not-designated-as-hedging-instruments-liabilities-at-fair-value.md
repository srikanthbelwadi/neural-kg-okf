  Value — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure indicates the fair value of all derivative liabilities that
  are not designated as hedging instruments for a publicly traded company or SEC filer.
  It reflects the potential obligations arising from these financial instruments,
  which can affect the company's financial position. This measure is distinct from
  other derivative measures as it focuses specifically on liabilities, rather than
  the overall fair value of derivatives. The value is reported in currency as of the
  balance sheet date.
