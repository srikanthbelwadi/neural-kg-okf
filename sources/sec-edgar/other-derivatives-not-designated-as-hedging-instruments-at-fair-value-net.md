  that are not designated as hedging instruments for a publicly traded company or
  SEC filer. It provides insight into the value of these financial instruments, which

  derivative measures as it specifically focuses on those not classified as hedging,
  highlighting their potential impact on financial performance. The value is reported
  in currency as of the balance sheet date.
