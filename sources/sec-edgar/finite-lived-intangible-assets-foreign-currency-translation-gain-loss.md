  or loss that affects finite-lived intangible assets for a publicly traded company.
  It quantifies how fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates impact the value

  measures as it excludes goodwill and capitalized costs for software intended for
  sale, lease, or marketing. The value is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
