description: Extinguishment of Debt, Gain (Loss), Net of Tax indicates the difference
  between the reacquisition price and the net carrying amount of extinguished debt,
  recognized as a component of income for the period, net of tax. This measure is
  relevant to publicly traded companies and reflects the financial impact of debt
  extinguishment on their income. It is distinct from other income measures as it
  specifically pertains to the net effect of extinguishing debt. The amount is reported
  in currency for the fiscal year.
