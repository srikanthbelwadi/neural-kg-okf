description: This measure represents the amount of noncash items, such as expenses
  or gains, that are adjusted to reconcile net income to cash provided by or used
  in operating activities when the indirect cash flow method is applied. It is relevant
  to publicly traded companies and provides insight into the differences between reported
  income and actual cash flow. This measure is distinct from cash flow measures that
  do not account for noncash adjustments, focusing specifically on reconciling net
  income. The value is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
