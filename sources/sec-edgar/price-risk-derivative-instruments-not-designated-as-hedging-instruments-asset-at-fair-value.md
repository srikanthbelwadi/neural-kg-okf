description: Price Risk Derivative Instruments Not Designated as Hedging Instruments
  Asset, at Fair Value measures the fair value of all price risk derivative assets
  that a publicly traded company holds but has not designated as hedging instruments.
  This measure provides insight into the company's exposure to price risk without
  the benefits of hedging. It is reported as an instant value in currency, differentiating
  it from designated hedging instruments.
