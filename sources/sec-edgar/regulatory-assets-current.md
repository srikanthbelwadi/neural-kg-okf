title: Regulatory Asset, Current — SEC EDGAR
description: 'Carrying amount as of the balance sheet date of capitalized costs of
  regulated entities that are expected to be recovered through revenue sources within
  one year or the normal operating cycle, if longer. Such costs are capitalized if
  they meet both of the following criteria: a. It is probable that future revenue
  in an amount at least equal to the capitalized cost will result from inclusion of
  that cost in allowable costs for rate-making purposes. b. Based on available evidence,
  the future revenue will be provided to permit recovery of the previously incurred
  cost rather than to provide for expected levels of similar future costs. If the
  revenue will be provided through an automatic rate-adjustment clause, this criterion
  requires that the regulator''s intent clearly be to permit recovery of the previously
  incurred cost.'
