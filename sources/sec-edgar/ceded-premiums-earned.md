title: Ceded Premiums Earned — SEC EDGAR
description: Ceded Premiums Earned reports the total amount of insurance premiums
  that a publicly traded company has earned but subsequently ceded to other entities,
  typically as part of a reinsurance agreement. This measure specifically focuses
  on the premiums that are transferred to other insurers, distinguishing it from total
  earned premiums which would include all premiums retained by the company. The value
  is reported in currency and reflects the duration of the premiums earned during
  the fiscal year.
