description: Extended Product Warranty Accrual, Period Increase (Decrease) captures
  the amount of increase or decrease in the extended product warranty liability over
  a reporting period. This measure is relevant to publicly traded companies that offer
  extended warranties and reflects changes in their financial obligations. It is distinguished
  from other warranty measures as it focuses on the period's changes rather than the
  total liability. The amount is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
