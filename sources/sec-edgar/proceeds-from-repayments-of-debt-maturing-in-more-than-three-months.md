description: Proceeds from (Repayments of) Debt, Maturing in More than Three Months
  reflects the net cash inflow from borrowings minus the cash outflow from repayments
  for debt with an initial term of repayment exceeding three months for a publicly
  traded company. This measure combines inflows and outflows, distinguishing it from
  measures that focus solely on inflows or outflows. It is reported as a duration
  value in currency.
