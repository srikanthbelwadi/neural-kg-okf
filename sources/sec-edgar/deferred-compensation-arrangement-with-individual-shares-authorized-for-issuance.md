  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure indicates the maximum number of shares authorized for issuance
  under a deferred compensation arrangement with an individual as of the balance sheet
  date. It reflects the potential equity that may be granted in the future as part
  of the compensation plan. This measure is distinct from other deferred compensation
  measures as it specifically addresses share authorization, rather than issued shares
  or expenses. The value is reported in shares for the organization at the balance
  sheet date.
