description: Banker's Acceptance, Due from Customer, before Allowance for Credit Loss
  reflects the total amount receivable from a customer for a banker's acceptance,
  without any adjustments for credit losses. This measure is relevant to a publicly
  traded company and provides a gross view of receivables, distinguishing it from
  net measures that account for potential losses. It is reported as an instant value
  in currency, indicating the balance at the reporting date.
