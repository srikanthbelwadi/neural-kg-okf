description: This measure reports the total amount of investment that is associated
  with a disposal group, which is a collection of assets and liabilities that are
  held for sale or have already been disposed of. The subject of this measure is a
  publicly traded company or SEC filer, specifically focusing on their investments
  in discontinued operations. It is distinct from other investment measures as it
  specifically pertains to those investments that are part of a disposal group, rather
  than general investments or those not related to discontinued operations. The value
  is reported as an instant value in currency.
