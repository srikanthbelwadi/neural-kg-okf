  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure indicates the amount of unrealized gain or loss recognized
  in income from liabilities measured at fair value on a recurring basis, specifically
  using unobservable inputs (level 3) and still held. It is applicable to publicly
  traded companies and SEC filers that manage such liabilities. This measure is distinct
  from other fair value measures as it specifically focuses on unrealized gains or
  losses from level 3 liabilities, rather than overall fair value changes. The value
  is reported as a duration value in currency.
