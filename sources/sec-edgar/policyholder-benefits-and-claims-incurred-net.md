title: Policyholder Benefits and Claims Incurred, Net — SEC EDGAR
description: Policyholder Benefits and Claims Incurred, Net represents the amount
  of expense related to the provision for policy benefits and costs incurred, adjusted
  for the effects of policies assumed or ceded. This measure is relevant to publicly
  traded insurance companies and reflects their net obligations to policyholders.
  It is distinguished from gross measures as it accounts for the net effect of reinsurance
  transactions. The amount is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
