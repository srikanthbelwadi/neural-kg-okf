  sheet. This measure is relevant to publicly traded companies and is used to adjust
  net income to arrive at net cash flows from operations. It is distinguished from
  other reserve measures by its specific focus on commissions and taxes rather than
  general reserves. The amount is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
