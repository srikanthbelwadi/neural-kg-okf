description: This measure indicates the fair value as of the balance sheet date of
  derivative instruments not designated as hedging instruments and classified as an
  asset by a publicly traded company. It provides insight into the company's speculative
  or trading positions in derivatives. This measure is distinct from hedging instruments,
  which are specifically used to mitigate risk, focusing instead on non-hedging positions.
  It is reported as an instant value in currency.
