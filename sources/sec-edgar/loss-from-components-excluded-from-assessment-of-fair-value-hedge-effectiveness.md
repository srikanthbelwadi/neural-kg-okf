  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure captures the loss related to components of a fair value
  hedging instrument that have been excluded from the assessment of hedge effectiveness.
  It applies to a publicly traded company or SEC filer, providing insight into the
  financial effects of hedging strategies on earnings. This measure is distinct from
  other loss metrics as it focuses specifically on fair value hedges, rather than
  cash flow hedges or operational losses. The data is reported in currency for the
  organization per fiscal year.
