description: This measure reports the weighted average purchase price of capital shares
  acquired through an employee stock ownership plan (ESOP) for a publicly traded company.
  It reflects the average cost per share that employees paid for their ownership stake,
  calculated by considering the total cost of shares purchased divided by the number
  of shares. This measure is distinct from other stock purchase metrics as it specifically
  pertains to shares acquired through an ESOP, and is reported in currency per share.
