  Expense — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reports the total amount of general and administrative expenses
  that are specifically associated with a disposal group, which may include discontinued
  operations. The subject of this measure is a publicly traded company or SEC filer,
  focusing on expenses that are incurred during the process of disposing of certain
  business segments. It is distinct from other expense measures as it specifically
  pertains to general and administrative costs rather than operational or production
  costs. The value is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
