description: Debt Securities, Held-to-Maturity, Restricted indicates the amount of
  restricted investment in debt securities that are measured at amortized cost and
  intended to be held until maturity. This measure is relevant to publicly traded
  companies and focuses on specific debt securities that have restrictions on their
  use. It is distinguished from other securities measures by its emphasis on held-to-maturity
  securities rather than those available for sale or trading. The amount is reported
  in currency as of the balance sheet date.
