description: This measure reports the total amount of obligations incurred by a publicly
  traded company or SEC filer for employer-related costs, classified as other and
  payable within one year or the normal operating cycle, if longer. It specifically
  addresses liabilities related to employee costs that do not fall under more specific
  categories. This measure is narrower than total employee-related liabilities, as
  it excludes specific types of employee obligations. The value is reported as an
  instant value in currency.
