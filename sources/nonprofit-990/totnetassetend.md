description: Net assets or fund balances at the end of the year represent the difference
  between total assets and total liabilities for a nonprofit organization as of the
  end of its fiscal year. This measure indicates the organization's financial position
  and sustainability, showing how much is left after all obligations are met. It is
  distinct from total assets and total liabilities, as it provides a net figure rather
  than gross amounts. The unit of measurement is in dollars, reported per organization
  per fiscal year.
