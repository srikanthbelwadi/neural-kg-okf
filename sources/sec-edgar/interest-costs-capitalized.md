description: This measure captures the amount of interest that has been capitalized
  during the reporting period for a publicly traded company. It reflects the interest
  costs that are added to the value of an asset rather than being expensed, providing
  insight into the company's investment in long-term assets. This measure is narrower
  than total interest costs incurred, as it specifically focuses on the portion of
  interest that is capitalized, excluding interest charged against earnings. The amount
  is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
