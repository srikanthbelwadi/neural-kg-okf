  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reports the total amount classified as accounts payable
  that is attributable to a disposal group, expected to be disposed of within one
  year or the normal operating cycle. The subject of this measure is a publicly traded
  company or SEC filer, focusing on current liabilities related to a disposal group.
  It is distinguished from other accounts payable measures by its specific association
  with disposal groups rather than general accounts payable. The value is reported
  in currency as an instant value.
