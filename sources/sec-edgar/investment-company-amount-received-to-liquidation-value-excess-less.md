title: Investment Company, Share, Amount Received to Liquidation Value, Excess (Less)
  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure captures the amount received for shares issued by an investment
  company that exceeds (or is less than) the preference value in the event of involuntary
  liquidation of the same class of security prior to sale. It is relevant to publicly
  traded investment companies and reflects the financial implications of share pricing
  in liquidation scenarios. This measure is distinct from other liquidation-related
  measures as it specifically addresses share issuance rather than overall liquidation
  values. The reported value is in currency for the fiscal year.
