description: Increase (Decrease) in Debt Securities, Trading, and Equity Securities,
  FV-NI captures the amount of increase or decrease in investments in debt and equity
  securities that are measured at fair value, with changes recognized in net income.
  This measure is relevant to publicly traded companies and reflects their trading
  activities in financial markets. It is distinct from other investment measures by
  focusing specifically on securities classified as trading and subject to fair value
  accounting. The reporting is in currency for each fiscal year.
