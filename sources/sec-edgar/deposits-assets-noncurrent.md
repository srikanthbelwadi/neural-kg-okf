description: This measure captures the carrying value of deposits that have been transferred
  to third parties for security purposes, which are expected to be returned or applied
  towards payment after one year or beyond the operating cycle. It pertains to a publicly
  traded company or SEC filer and reflects noncurrent assets related to deposits.
  This measure is distinct from current deposits as it focuses on those that are not
  expected to be liquidated within the next year. The value is reported as an instant
  value in currency.
