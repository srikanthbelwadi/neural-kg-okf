  Net of Tax — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reports the net amount of gain or loss, after tax, that
  arises from the disposal of a discontinued operation. It specifically pertains to
  publicly traded companies or SEC filers that have classified certain operations
  as discontinued. This measure is distinct from other discontinued operation metrics
  as it focuses solely on the financial outcome of the disposal itself, rather than
  ongoing operations or provisions related to the discontinued operation. The value
  is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
