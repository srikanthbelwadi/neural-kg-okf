title: Reorganization Value — SEC EDGAR
description: Reorganization Value measures the amount attributed to a reconstituted
  entity, reflecting the expected net realizable value of assets that will be disposed
  of before the reconstitution occurs. This concept pertains to a publicly traded
  company or SEC filer undergoing a reorganization process. It is distinct from other
  asset measures as it specifically focuses on the value associated with reorganization,
  rather than general asset valuations. The value is reported as an instant value
  in currency.
