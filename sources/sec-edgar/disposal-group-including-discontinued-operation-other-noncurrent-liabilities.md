title: Disposal Group, Including Discontinued Operation, Other Liabilities, Noncurrent
  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure indicates the amount classified as other noncurrent liabilities
  related to a disposal group that is either held for sale or has been disposed of,
  with an expectation of disposal beyond one year. It is relevant to publicly traded
  companies involved in such transactions. This measure is distinct from current liabilities
  as it specifically addresses noncurrent liabilities within a disposal context. The
  amount is reported in currency as an instant value.
