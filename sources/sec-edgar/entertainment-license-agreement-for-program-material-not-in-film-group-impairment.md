  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reports the amount of impairment loss on capitalized costs
  for rights acquired by a broadcaster under a license agreement for program material
  not included in the film group. It reflects the financial impact of impairments
  on specific broadcasting rights, specifically focusing on those not categorized
  within the film group. This measure is distinct from other impairment measures as
  it pertains specifically to non-film group program material and is reported as a
  duration value in currency.
