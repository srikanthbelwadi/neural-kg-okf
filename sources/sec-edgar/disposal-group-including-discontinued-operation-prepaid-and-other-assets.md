title: Disposal Group, Including Discontinued Operation, Prepaid and Other Assets
  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reflects the total amount classified as prepaid and other
  assets attributable to a disposal group held for sale or already disposed of by
  a publicly traded company. Unlike the current measure, it does not specify a time
  frame for disposal, thus encompassing all prepaid and other assets regardless of
  their expected disposal timeline. This distinction allows for a comprehensive view
  of asset holdings related to disposal groups, without the short-term focus of current
  measures. The value is reported in currency as an instant value.
