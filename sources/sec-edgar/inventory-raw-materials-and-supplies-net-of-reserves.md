title: Inventory, Raw Materials and Supplies, Net of Reserves — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure aggregates the carrying amount of raw materials and supplies
  that a publicly traded company has, net of valuation reserves and adjustments, as
  of the balance sheet date. It includes both unprocessed materials for manufacturing
  and supplies that will be consumed in operations. This measure is distinct from
  other inventory measures as it combines raw materials with supplies, providing a
  broader view of inventory used in production. The value is reported in currency.
