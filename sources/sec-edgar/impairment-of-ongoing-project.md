title: Impairment of Ongoing Project — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reflects the amount recognized as an operating expense or
  loss during the period to reduce the carrying amount of an ongoing project that
  has been impaired but not abandoned. It pertains to publicly traded companies that
  report on their project valuations and impairments. This measure is distinct from
  other impairment measures as it specifically addresses ongoing projects rather than
  assets that have been fully abandoned or disposed of. The value is reported in currency
  for each fiscal year.
