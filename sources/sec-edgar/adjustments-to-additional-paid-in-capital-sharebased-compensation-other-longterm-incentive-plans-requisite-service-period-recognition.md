title: APIC, Share-Based Payment Arrangement, Other, Increase for Cost Recognition
  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure indicates the increase to additional paid-in capital (APIC)
  for the recognition of costs related to awards classified as other under share-based
  payment arrangements. It applies to publicly traded companies and captures the accounting
  for various long-term incentive plans that do not fall under standard stock options
  or restricted stock units. Unlike other APIC adjustments, this measure specifically
  addresses awards categorized as 'other,' providing a broader scope of share-based
  compensation. The value is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
