description: Grandfathered ESOP Expense Recognition, Cash Contributions to ESOP captures
  the total cash contributions made to an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP) for
  shares acquired prior to January 1, 1993, by a publicly traded company. This measure
  provides insight into the funding of the ESOP and the company's commitment to employee
  ownership. It is distinguished from other ESOP-related measures by focusing solely
  on cash contributions rather than interest or dividends. The value is reported as
  a duration value in currency.
