description: Increase (Decrease) of Restricted Investments measures the net cash inflow
  or outflow related to investments that are pledged or subject to withdrawal restrictions,
  excluding restricted cash. This measure pertains to publicly traded companies and
  provides insight into their investment strategies involving restricted assets. It
  is differentiated from other investment measures by its focus on restricted investments,
  which may have limitations on liquidity or access. The reporting is expressed in
  currency for each fiscal year.
