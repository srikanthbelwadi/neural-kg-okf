description: Retained Earnings, Undistributed Earnings from Equity Method Investees
  represents the amount of consolidated retained earnings that are derived from undistributed
  earnings of equity method investees, which are entities owned 50 percent or less.
  This measure specifically pertains to the accounting for equity method investments,
  distinguishing it from retained earnings derived from wholly-owned subsidiaries.
  It is reported as an instant value in currency, reflecting the amount at the balance
  sheet date.
