title: Deferred Tax Assets, Valuation Allowance — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reports the amount of deferred tax assets for which it is
  more likely than not that a tax benefit will not be realized. It pertains to a publicly
  traded company or SEC filer and indicates the potential limitations on recognizing
  deferred tax assets. This measure is distinct from other deferred tax asset measures
  that report amounts expected to be realized, as it specifically addresses those
  assets deemed less likely to provide future tax benefits. The value is reported
  as an instant value in currency.
