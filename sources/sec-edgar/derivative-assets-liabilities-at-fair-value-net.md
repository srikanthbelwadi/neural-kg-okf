description: This measure reports the fair values as of the balance sheet date of
  the net amount of all assets and liabilities resulting from contracts that meet
  the criteria of being accounted for as derivative instruments by a publicly traded
  company. It provides a comprehensive view of the company's derivative positions
  and their financial implications. This measure is distinct from individual asset
  or liability measures, focusing instead on the net position. It is reported as an
  instant value in currency.
