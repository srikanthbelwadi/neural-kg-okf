description: This measure reports the percentage of an entity's employees covered
  by a collective-bargaining arrangement (CBA) in relation to all employees in a multiemployer
  pension plan determined to be individually significant. It is relevant for publicly
  traded companies that participate in multiemployer pension plans and assess employee
  coverage. This measure is distinct from other employee coverage percentages as it
  focuses on the overall percentage of employees under CBAs, rather than those considered
  significant. The value is reported as a percentage over a duration.
