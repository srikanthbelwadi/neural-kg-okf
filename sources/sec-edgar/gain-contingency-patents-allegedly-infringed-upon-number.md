description: This measure reports the number of patents held by a publicly traded
  company or SEC filer that another entity has allegedly infringed, indicating potential
  legal claims against the infringing party. It reflects the company's position in
  intellectual property disputes. This measure is distinct from other gain contingencies
  as it specifically addresses alleged infringements, rather than those confirmed
  as infringed or not infringed, and is reported as a pure count.
