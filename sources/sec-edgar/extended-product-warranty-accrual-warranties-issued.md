description: Extended Product Warranty Accrual, Increase for Warranties Issued measures
  the increase in the extended product warranty accrual resulting from warranties
  that have been issued. This measure is relevant to publicly traded companies that
  offer extended warranties and reflects their financial obligations related to these
  warranties. It is distinguished from standard product warranties as it focuses solely
  on extended warranties. The amount is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
