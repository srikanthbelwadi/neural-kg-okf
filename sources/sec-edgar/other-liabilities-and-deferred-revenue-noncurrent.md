title: Other Liabilities and Deferred Revenue, Noncurrent — SEC EDGAR
description: Aggregate carrying amount, as of the balance sheet date, of noncurrent
  obligations and noncurrent portion of deferred revenue not separately disclosed
  in the balance sheet. Noncurrent liabilities are expected to be paid after one year
  (or the normal operating cycle, if longer). Deferred revenue is a liability related
  to a revenue producing activity for which revenue has not yet been recognized, and
  is not expected to be recognized in the next twelve months.
