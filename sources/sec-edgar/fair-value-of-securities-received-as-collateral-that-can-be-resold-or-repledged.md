title: Fair Value of Securities Received as Collateral that Can be Resold or Repledged
  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure indicates the fair value of securities that a publicly traded
  company has received as collateral and is permitted to resell or re-pledge, either
  by contract or customary practice. It reflects the potential liquidity available
  to the company from these collateralized securities. Unlike other collateral measures,
  this one specifically focuses on the fair value of securities that can be actively
  managed or utilized for additional financing. The value is reported as an instant
  value in currency.
