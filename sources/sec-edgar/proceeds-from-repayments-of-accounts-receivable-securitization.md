description: This measure indicates the proceeds from repayments of securitizations
  of receivables treated as collateralized borrowings for a publicly traded company.
  It provides insight into the cash inflows related to the repayment of these securitized
  assets, which can affect the company's liquidity. This measure is distinct from
  proceeds from initial securitizations, as it focuses specifically on repayments,
  offering a different perspective on the company's financing activities. The value
  is reported as a duration value in currency.
