title: Short-Term Debt, Percentage Bearing Variable Interest Rate — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reports the percentage of short-term borrowings that are
  subject to variable interest rates as of the balance sheet date for a publicly traded
  company. It specifically counts the portion of the carrying amount of these borrowings
  that can fluctuate over time based on market conditions. This measure is distinct
  from total short-term debt, as it focuses solely on the interest rate aspect rather
  than the overall amount of debt. It is reported as an instant value in percent.
