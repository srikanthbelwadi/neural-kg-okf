description: Assumed Premiums Earned indicates the total amount of insurance premiums
  that a publicly traded company has earned from other entities, typically through
  reinsurance agreements. This measure specifically captures the premiums that the
  company has taken on from other insurers, differentiating it from ceded premiums
  which are those transferred away. The value is reported in currency and reflects
  the duration of the premiums earned during the fiscal year.
