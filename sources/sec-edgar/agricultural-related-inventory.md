description: This measure indicates the total carrying amount of inventory related
  to agricultural products, specifically those associated with plants and animals,
  as of the balance sheet date. It applies to publicly traded companies and SEC filers,
  reflecting the value of goods held for sale or use in agricultural operations. Unlike
  broader inventory measures, this specifically excludes non-agricultural items and
  is reported as an instant value in currency.
