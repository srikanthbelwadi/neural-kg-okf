description: Intangible Asset, Excluding Goodwill, after Accumulated Amortization
  measures the amount of finite- and indefinite-lived intangible assets, excluding
  goodwill, after accounting for accumulated amortization. This measure applies to
  publicly traded companies and provides insight into their intangible asset valuation.
  It is distinguished from other asset measures by its exclusion of goodwill, focusing
  instead on other intangible assets. The reporting is in currency as an instant value.
