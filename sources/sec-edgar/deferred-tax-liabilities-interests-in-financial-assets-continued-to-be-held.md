  to taxable temporary differences from interests in financial instruments that are
  continued to be held after the sale or transfer of related financial instruments.
  It describes the future tax obligations of a publicly traded company or SEC filer
  resulting from the timing differences in accounting and tax treatment of these interests.
  This measure is specific to financial instruments and differs from other deferred
  tax liabilities that may relate to different asset classes. The value is reported
  as an instant value in currency.
