title: Income (Loss) from Ongoing Equity Method Investment in Discontinued Operation
  after Disposal — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure captures the share of income or loss from an equity method
  investee that a publicly traded company continues to hold after the disposal of
  a discontinued operation. It reflects the ongoing financial performance of the investee
  post-disposal, distinguishing it from measures that account for the entire income
  from discontinued operations. This measure is specifically focused on the ongoing
  equity investment and does not include any prior income or loss from the discontinued
  operation itself. The value is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
