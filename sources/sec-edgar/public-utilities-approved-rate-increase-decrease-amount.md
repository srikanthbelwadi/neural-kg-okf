description: Public Utilities Approved Rate Increase (Decrease) Amount indicates the
  total dollar amount of a public utility's rate increase or decrease that has been
  approved by a regulatory agency. This measure is essential for understanding the
  financial adjustments that have received regulatory consent, distinguishing it from
  requested amounts that may not have been granted. The value is reported in currency
  and reflects the duration of the approved changes.
