  — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reports the amount classified as deferred revenue attributable
  to a disposal group held for sale or already disposed of by a publicly traded company,
  specifically focusing on revenue expected to be recognized beyond one year or the
  normal operating cycle, if longer. This distinguishes it from current deferred revenue
  measures, which focus on short-term expectations. It provides insight into long-term
  revenue obligations related to disposal groups, highlighting future income potential.
  The value is reported in currency as an instant value.
