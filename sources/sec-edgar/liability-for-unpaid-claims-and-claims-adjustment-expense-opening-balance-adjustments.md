  claims adjustment expenses. This measure is applicable to publicly traded companies
  and reflects changes in liabilities related to claims. It is distinct from other
  liability measures by its specific focus on claims and adjustments rather than general
  liabilities. The value is reported in currency as of the balance sheet date.
