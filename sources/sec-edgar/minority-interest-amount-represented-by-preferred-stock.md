description: Noncontrolling Interest, Amount Represented by Preferred Stock quantifies
  the portion of noncontrolling interest in a subsidiary that is represented by preferred
  securities issued by that subsidiary. This measure is relevant for publicly traded
  companies with subsidiaries that have issued preferred stock. It is distinct from
  general noncontrolling interest measures as it specifically focuses on preferred
  stock, which may have different rights and privileges. The value is reported as
  an instant value in currency.
