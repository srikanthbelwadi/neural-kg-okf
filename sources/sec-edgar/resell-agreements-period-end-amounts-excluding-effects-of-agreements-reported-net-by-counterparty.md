description: Resell Agreements, Period End Amounts Excluding Effects of Agreements
  Reported Net by Counterparty captures the total amount of resale agreements at the
  reporting date, presented as a gross figure. This measure is relevant to publicly
  traded companies and provides insight into their resale agreements without netting
  amounts by counterparty. It is distinguished from other agreement measures by its
  focus on gross amounts rather than net figures, offering a clearer view of total
  agreements. The amount is reported in currency as of the reporting date.
