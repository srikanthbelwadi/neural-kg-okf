description: Partners' Capital, Including Portion Attributable to Noncontrolling Interest
  represents the total amount of partners' capital, including contributions from both
  the parent and noncontrolling interests. This measure is relevant to publicly traded
  companies and their equity structure. It is distinguished from other equity measures
  by including noncontrolling interests and excluding temporary equity. The reported
  value is in currency as of the balance sheet date.
