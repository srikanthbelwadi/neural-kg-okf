description: The noncurrent portion of the carrying value as of the balance sheet
  date of a type of municipal bond typically used to fund a development project. Also
  called special assessment limited liability bond, special district bond, special
  purpose bond, special tax bond and community development obligation. Interest owed
  to lenders is paid by taxes levied on the community benefiting from the particular
  bond-funded project. For example, if a bond of this sort was issued to pay for sidewalks
  to be repaved in a certain community, an additional tax would be levied on homeowners
  in the area benefiting from this project.
