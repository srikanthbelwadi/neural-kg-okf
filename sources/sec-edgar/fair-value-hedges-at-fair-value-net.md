description: This measure reports the net fair value of all derivative instruments
  that a publicly traded company has designated as fair value hedging instruments.
  It specifically pertains to the company's hedging activities aimed at managing exposure
  to changes in the fair value of recognized assets or liabilities. This measure is
  distinct from other fair value measures as it focuses solely on derivatives used
  for hedging, rather than all derivatives or other financial instruments. The value
  is reported as an instant value in currency.
