description: This measure represents the amount classified as other assets that are
  part of a disposal group, which includes assets expected to be sold or disposed
  of after one year or the normal operating cycle, if longer. It pertains to a publicly
  traded company or SEC filer and reflects the financial implications of discontinued
  operations. This measure is distinct from current assets, as it focuses on noncurrent
  assets specifically tied to disposal activities. The value is reported as an instant
  value in currency.
