  Inputs Reconciliation, Transfers into Level 3 — SEC EDGAR
description: This measure reports the amount of transfers of financial instruments
  classified as derivative assets or liabilities into level 3 of the fair value hierarchy,
  after deducting any derivative liabilities. It is applicable to publicly traded
  companies and SEC filers that hold such financial instruments. This measure is distinct
  from other fair value measures as it specifically addresses transfers into level
  3, which involves unobservable inputs, rather than overall fair value changes. The
  value is reported as a duration value in currency.
