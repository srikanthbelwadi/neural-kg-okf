description: Undistributed Earnings, Basic indicates the earnings allocated to common
  stock and participating securities, reflecting the portion of earnings that would
  be distributed if all earnings were paid out. This measure is applicable to publicly
  traded companies and their earnings distribution policies. It is distinguished from
  other earnings measures by focusing on earnings that are not yet distributed rather
  than total earnings or net income. The reported value is in currency for the fiscal
  year.
