description: Payments for Repurchase of Preferred Stock and Preference Stock indicates
  the cash outflow incurred when a company reacquires its preferred stock during a
  given period. This measure is applicable to publicly traded companies and specifically
  addresses transactions involving preferred stock, which typically has different
  rights and privileges compared to common stock. It is distinct from other repurchase
  measures by focusing exclusively on preferred stock rather than common stock or
  other financial instruments. The value is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
