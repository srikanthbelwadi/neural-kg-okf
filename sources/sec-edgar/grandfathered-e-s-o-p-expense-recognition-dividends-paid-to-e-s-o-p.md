description: Grandfathered ESOP Expense Recognition, Dividends Paid to ESOP reflects
  the total dividends distributed to an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP) for shares
  acquired before January 1, 1993, by a publicly traded company. This measure indicates
  the financial returns provided to employee shareholders through the ESOP. It is
  distinct from other ESOP expense measures as it specifically pertains to dividends
  rather than contributions or interest costs. The value is reported as a duration
  value in currency.
