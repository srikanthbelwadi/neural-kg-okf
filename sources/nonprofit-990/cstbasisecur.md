description: Cost or other basis of securities sold measures the original cost or
  basis of securities that a nonprofit organization has sold. This financial field
  is specific to securities transactions and does not include costs associated with
  other types of assets or operational expenses. It is distinct from gain/loss on
  securities sales, as it focuses on the cost rather than the financial outcome of
  the sale. The reporting is done per organization for each fiscal year, in dollars.
