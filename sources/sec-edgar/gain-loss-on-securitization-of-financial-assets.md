title: Gain (Loss) on Securitization of Financial Assets — SEC EDGAR
description: A gain (loss) realized on a transfer, accounted for as a sale, of all
  or a portion of financial assets in which the transferor surrenders control and
  receives consideration other than a beneficial interest in the assets transferred.
  Reflects the amount of sales proceeds in excess of, or deficient from, the sum of
  the carrying amounts of transferred financial assets plus transaction costs.
