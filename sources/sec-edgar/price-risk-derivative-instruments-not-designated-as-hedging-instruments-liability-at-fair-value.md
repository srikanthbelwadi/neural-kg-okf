description: Price Risk Derivative Instruments Not Designated as Hedging Instruments
  Liability, at Fair Value represents the fair value of all price risk derivative
  liabilities that a publicly traded company holds but has not designated as hedging
  instruments as of the balance sheet date. This measure is crucial for understanding
  the company's financial obligations related to these derivatives. It is reported
  as an instant value in currency, differentiating it from assets or designated hedging
  instruments.
