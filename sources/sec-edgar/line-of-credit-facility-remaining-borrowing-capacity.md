  under a line of credit facility for a publicly traded company, calculated as the
  total borrowing capacity minus any outstanding borrowings. It is reported as an
  instant value in currency, providing a snapshot of the funds that can still be accessed.
  This measure is distinct from the maximum borrowing capacity, as it reflects the
  current available funds rather than the theoretical limit.
