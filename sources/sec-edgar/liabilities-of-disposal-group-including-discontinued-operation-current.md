  indicates the total liabilities associated with a disposal group held for sale or
  disposed of by a publicly traded company, expected to be settled within one year
  or the normal operating cycle. This measure specifically focuses on liabilities
  related to discontinued operations, distinguishing it from ongoing operational liabilities.
  The value is reported as an instant value in currency.
