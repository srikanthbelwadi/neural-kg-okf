description: This measure provides the weighted average grant-date fair value of non-vested
  options that are currently outstanding for a publicly traded company or SEC filer.
  It reflects the average value assigned to these options at the time they were granted,
  weighted by the number of options. This measure is distinct from those related to
  forfeited or vested options, as it focuses specifically on non-vested options that
  remain outstanding. The value is reported on a per-share basis.
