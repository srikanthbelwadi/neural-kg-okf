description: This measure indicates the amount of income or loss generated by an equity
  method investee that a publicly traded company retains after disposing of a discontinued
  operation, before accounting for income tax. It reflects the financial performance
  of the investee during the period following the disposal, providing insight into
  the ongoing financial relationship. This measure is specifically about retained
  equity investments and is not a comprehensive view of all income from discontinued
  operations. The value is reported in currency for the fiscal year.
