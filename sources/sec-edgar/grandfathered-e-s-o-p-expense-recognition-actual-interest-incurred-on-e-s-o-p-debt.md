description: Grandfathered ESOP Expense Recognition, Actual Interest Incurred on ESOP
  Debt measures the actual interest costs incurred on debt obligations secured by
  the employer for an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP) for shares acquired before
  January 1, 1993. This measure is relevant for publicly traded companies with grandfathered
  ESOPs, providing insight into the financial burden of ESOP-related debt. It is distinct
  from other ESOP expense measures as it specifically focuses on interest costs rather
  than contributions or dividends. The value is reported as a duration value in currency.
