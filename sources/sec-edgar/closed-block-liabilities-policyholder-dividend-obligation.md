title: Closed Block Dividend Obligation — SEC EDGAR
description: The liability as of the balance sheet date for the excess of the cumulative
  actual closed block earnings over the cumulative expected (actuarially determined)
  closed block earnings (each) for the period from the actuarial calculation date
  to the date of measurement. The excess will result in future dividends to closed
  block policyholders, unless offset by less favorable than expected future performance
  of the closed block. The policyholder dividend obligation, which may not have a
  negative balance, recognizes that a portion of earnings in certain cases will not
  inure to the stockholders of the insurance entity.
